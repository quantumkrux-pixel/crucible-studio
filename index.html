The forge, step by step

From a blank scene to a finished model, without leaving the page.

Crucible3D follows the arc of actually making something — each stage flows into the next, all in one browser tab.

01

Shape

Drop in cubes, spheres, cones, a stylized head, and more. Move, rotate, and scale with direct touch or mouse control.

02

Sculpt

Push and pull faces, deform vertices, slice, hollow, mirror, and merge. Draw freehand 3D tubes with the Magic Pen.

03

Animate

Pose your objects frame by frame like claymation. Onion-skinning shows the last pose while you set the next.

04

Export

Send your work out as GLB, OBJ, or FBX — with materials, textures, transparency, and animation baked in. Ready for Blender, Unity, or a 3D printer.

Your phone is a modeling studio now.

Crucible3D was built mobile-first, not shrunk down as an afterthought. The interface adapts to a touch dock and sheets on a phone, and a full rail and panels on a desktop — same project, same files, either device.

  • Touch-native orbit, pan, and pinch-zoom
  • Long-press any object to grab and move it
  • Install it to your home screen like an app

It's yours — projects, textures, the works.

Paint objects with imported image textures. Set per-object transparency for glass and ghosts. Control the lighting. Keep as many projects as you like, each with its own look, synced to your account or saved right to your device.

  • Image textures with automatic UV mapping
  • Per-project lighting and color themes
  • Undo everything · nothing is precious
