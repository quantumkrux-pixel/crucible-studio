The pitch
"Forge 3D models in the tab you already have open."

Most 3D tools ask you to download gigabytes, learn a dense professional interface, and sit at a desktop workstation. Crucible3D is the opposite bet: a genuinely capable modeling and animation studio that loads instantly in a browser, adapts to touch on a phone, and lets anyone curious start shaping something in seconds — then export it to the professional formats the rest of the 3D world already uses.

What you can do

Model & sculpt

  • Eight base shapes including a stylized head to start figures
  • Move, rotate, scale with touch or mouse; long-press to grab
  • Mesh tools: deform vertices, push/pull faces, slice, hollow
  • More tools: mirror, flip, clone, merge multiple objects
  • Magic Pen — draw freehand 3D tubes in space

Finish & animate

  • Image textures with automatic UV mapping
  • Per-object transparency and per-project lighting
  • Stop-motion timeline — pose frame by frame
  • Onion-skinning to line up each next pose
  • Export animated GLB for Blender, Unity, Unreal

Boilerplate

Crucible3D is a browser-based 3D modeling and stop-motion animation studio that runs on any modern device without an install. It combines primitive and mesh modeling tools, freehand 3D drawing, image texturing, per-project lighting, and a frame-by-frame animation timeline, and exports to GLB, OBJ, and FBX. The full studio is free to try with one saved project; a one-time $10 purchase unlocks unlimited projects, exporting, and an installable offline build.
